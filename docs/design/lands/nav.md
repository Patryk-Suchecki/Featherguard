# Nav

How the fights in Nav play. A land file never decides the roster: which enemies appear here is set by the Bestiary in [`GDD.md`](../../GDD.md#bestiary), and their stages are in [`enemies.md`](../enemies.md). It never covers story missions or side activities either; those belong to [`world-progression.md`](../world-progression.md).

Only the theme is written so far; the other sections come with the land's spec.

## Theme

![Nav battle backdrop concept](../../../Art/Concepts/Nav.png)

Concept: `Art/Concepts/Nav.png`.

- **Motif:** flat ash-grey ground at the end of the Kalinov Bridge, in the land of the dead, in an endless grey twilight. Everything is drained of colour and silent; the dead drift in the fog.
- **Layout:** the ash-grey clearing is the stage; heroes stand on the left, by the bridge they came over, enemies on the right. Around it: the end of the Kalinov Bridge over the smoking Smorodina river on the left, leaning roofed crosses and gravestones, bone-white dead trees and a broken stone gate on the right, clay pots and dried flowers left as offerings. Behind: a grey plain fading into fog, with pale ghost figures far away.
- **Folk details:** the Kalinov Bridge and the Smorodina, the border of the dead in Slavic tales, and the offerings at the graves.
- **Palette and light:** the Nav palette in [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](../../STYLE_GUIDE.md#nav): ash and bone, cold fog and a flat grey light from above. The ghost light is the only saturated colour.
- **What the 3D backdrop should do differently from the concept:**
  - the battle camera is side-on at a slight angle, so the clearing is shallower and the characters fill more of the frame;
  - the ghosts in the distance and the river smoke are effects; fallen units rising as ghosts (the Law) use the same look;
  - the graves and the bridge rail in the foreground stay low, so they never cover the characters' feet or the timing rings.

## Law

**Chill of Nav** (from the GDD): healing is halved; the fallen rise as ghosts unless finished with fire. How it changes these fights comes with the land's spec.
