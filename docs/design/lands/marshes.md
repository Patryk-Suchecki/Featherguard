# Marshes

How the fights in the Marshes play. A land file never decides the roster: which enemies appear here is set by the Bestiary in [`GDD.md`](../../GDD.md#bestiary), and their stages are in [`enemies.md`](../enemies.md). It never covers story missions or side activities either; those belong to [`world-progression.md`](../world-progression.md).

Only the theme is written so far; the other sections come with the land's spec.

## Theme

![Marshes battle backdrop concept](../../../Art/Concepts/Marshes.png)

Concept: `Art/Concepts/Marshes.png`.

- **Motif:** a patch of firm, mossy, half-sunken ground in black still water at dusk. The water feels deep and watched; anything could rise from it, because this is the land of the Water Maiden.
- **Layout:** the mossy island is the stage; heroes stand on the left, enemies on the right. Around it: tall reeds and bulrushes in big clumps, a rotten wooden jetty in the foreground, two weeping willows, a half-sunk boat and an old idol post wrapped in rotting ribbons. Further back a foggy line of dead trees under a heavy violet-grey sky.
- **Folk details:** the ribboned idol post and the boat, hints at the drowned and at the Water Maiden.
- **Palette and light:** the Marshes palette in [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](../../STYLE_GUIDE.md#marshes): black water, mud, reeds and moss, a violet-grey dusk sky and cold pale light from the heroes' side. The pale blue-green wisps are the only saturated colour.
- **What the 3D backdrop should do differently from the concept:**
  - the battle camera is side-on at a slight angle, so the island is shallower and the characters fill more of the frame;
  - leave open water near the enemies' side where submerging enemies can sink and rise, so submerging enemies read in the scene;
  - the wisps are effects, not part of the backdrop model;
  - the foreground jetty and reeds stay low, so they never cover the characters' feet or the timing rings.

## Enemy ideas

Lands have no Laws: these fights differ by their enemies. The former Law becomes an idea for enemy and boss abilities here: enemies that submerge, and wisps that switch targets. How they play comes with the land's enemy sheets.
