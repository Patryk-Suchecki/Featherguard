# Bald Mountain

How the fights in Bald Mountain play. A land file never decides the roster: which enemies appear here is set by the Bestiary in [`GDD.md`](../../GDD.md#bestiary), and their stages are in [`enemies.md`](../enemies.md). It never covers story missions or side activities either; those belong to [`world-progression.md`](../world-progression.md).

Only the theme is written so far; the other sections come with the land's spec.

## Theme

![Bald Mountain battle backdrop concept](../../../Art/Concepts/BaldMountain.png)

Concept: `Art/Concepts/BaldMountain.png`.

- **Motif:** a flat plateau of cracked dark stone near the summit at night, in a storm. A witches' sabbath place, with the Dragoness' fire glowing on a far peak.
- **Layout:** the cracked plateau is the stage; heroes stand on the left, enemies on the right. Around it: a ring of standing stones, jagged rocks at the edges, wind-bent dead pines, and on the left a cold sabbath fire pit with bones, broken brooms and a goat skull on a pole. Behind: more peaks fading into storm clouds, one with an orange glow of dragon fire.
- **Folk details:** the sabbath fire pit, the brooms and the goat skull, hints at the witches who gather here.
- **Palette and light:** the Bald Mountain palette in [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](../../STYLE_GUIDE.md#bald-mountain): dark basalt and grey rock under a storm sky, cold lightning from above, the dragon fire as the only warm colour.
- **What the 3D backdrop should do differently from the concept:**
  - the battle camera is side-on at a slight angle, so the plateau is shallower and the characters fill more of the frame;
  - the lightning is an effect that strikes when the Law does, not a painted bolt; between strikes the sky only flickers;
  - the standing stones in the foreground stay low, so they never cover the characters' feet or the timing rings.

## Law

**Storm** (from the GDD): lightning strikes a random character; dodge it in a QTE. How it changes these fights comes with the land's spec.
