# Old Forest

How the fights in the Old Forest play. A land file never decides the roster: which enemies appear here is set by the Bestiary in [`GDD.md`](../../GDD.md#bestiary), and their stages are in [`enemies.md`](../enemies.md). It never covers story missions or side activities either; those belong to [`world-progression.md`](../world-progression.md).

Only the theme is written so far; the other sections come with the land's spec.

## Theme

![Old Forest battle backdrop concept](../../../Art/Concepts/OldForest.png)

Concept: `Art/Concepts/OldForest.png`.

- **Motif:** a small glade of moss and roots among enormous ancient trees at night. The forest is alive and watching; faces hide in the bark and eyes glow in the dark, because this is the land of Lady Leshy.
- **Layout:** the bare glade is the stage; heroes stand on the left, enemies on the right. Around it: huge twisted trunks with faces in the bark, arching roots, big pale mushrooms, a spider web between two trees, carved wooden wards and a cracked offering bowl on a stump, a hollow tree with a green witch light. Further back the trunks close in, with cold moonlight falling into the glade.
- **Folk details:** the carved wards and the offering bowl, left for the forest spirit.
- **Palette and light:** the Old Forest palette in [`STYLE_GUIDE.md`](../../STYLE_GUIDE.md#old-forest): dark bark, roots and moss, pale mushrooms, cold moonlight from above. The witch light and the eyes are the only saturated colours.
- **What the 3D backdrop should do differently from the concept:**
  - the battle camera is side-on at a slight angle, so the glade is shallower and the characters fill more of the frame;
  - leave dark gaps between the trunks on the enemies' side, where hidden enemies can wait unseen, so Misleading reads in the scene;
  - the glowing eyes and the witch light are effects;
  - roots and mushrooms in the foreground stay low, so they never cover the characters' feet or the timing rings.

## Law

**Misleading** (from the GDD): the party's formation shuffles; enemies stay hidden until Marked. How it changes these fights comes with the land's spec.
